Deposit

EMD funding requires a 5% upfront deposit of the total loan amount, with a $250 minimum. This deposit is non-refundable and is credited toward your total fee if the deal goes through. It is paid by Zelle or wire, with the remaining balance reflected in your paperwork. Because time is of the essence we typically use Zelle, but wire is always welcome.
