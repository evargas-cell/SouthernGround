## Why this article

The `/hard-money-loans/georgia` landing page targets the commercial query. This
article targets the informational one — the investor who is researching cost
before they are ready to talk to a lender. It should link into the landing page
rather than compete with it, and it must go deeper on numbers than the landing
page does.

## Outline

1. **What "hard money" actually means in Georgia**
   Asset-based, business-purpose, non-owner-occupied, closed in an entity.
   Distinguish from private money from an individual, and from a bank
   investment-property loan.

2. **What it costs in 2026 — the honest range**
   - Rates from 8.25% on fix and flip and bridge; DSCR from 5.39%
   - Points: 1–3% of loan amount
   - Third-party costs: appraisal, title, insurance, entity docs
   - A full worked example: $285K purchase / $65K rehab / $480K ARV, $308K loan
     at 88% LTC, 9.75% and 2 points, held six months. Show the total cost of
     capital in dollars, then show it as a share of the $94K profit.

3. **Leverage: LTC vs LTV vs ARV**
   Explain that fix and flip leverage is quoted against cost but capped against
   after-repair value, and that both tests have to clear. This is the single
   most misunderstood mechanic for new borrowers.

4. **What underwriting actually looks at in Georgia**
   - Submarket-specific comps (the metro's value gradient — link to the Atlanta page)
   - Line-item scope of work, not a lump sum
   - Track record
   - Entity documents

5. **Timelines, and the Georgia foreclosure calendar**
   First-Tuesday-of-the-month county sales; wholesale assignments with 10–14 day
   windows. Why the ability to close fast is a competitive advantage here
   specifically.

6. **How to qualify — the five-item checklist**
   Pull from the Fix & Flip program page qualification list, expanded.

7. **When hard money is the wrong tool**
   Thin margins, no clear exit, long stabilization horizons, owner-occupied.
   Being explicit about this builds more trust than a pure sales piece.

   - Are hard money lenders licensed in Georgia?
   - Can I use a hard money loan at a foreclosure auction?
