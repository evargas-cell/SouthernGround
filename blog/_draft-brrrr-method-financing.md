## Outline

1. **The five stages, and the loan that funds each**

   | Stage | What happens | Product |
   |---|---|---|
   | Buy | Acquire below market | Fix & Flip or Bridge |
   | Rehab | Renovate to rent-ready | Fix & Flip rehab holdback |
   | Rent | Lease and document income | no financing — the seasoning clock runs |
   | Refinance | Pull capital back out | DSCR or Cash-Out Refinance |
   | Repeat | Redeploy into the next deal | back to stage one |

2. **Stage 1 — Buy**
   Why a DSCR loan cannot fund this: the property is not rent-ready.
   Leverage quoted on cost, capped against ARV, what you bring to closing.

3. **Stage 2 — Rehab**
   The draw process. Front-loading your own capital into demo and rough trades.
   Requesting draws by completed line item rather than by percentage.
   Link to the rehab budget guide.

4. **Stage 3 — Rent**
   Lease it before you apply for the refinance. Market rent versus lease rate and
   the appraiser's 1007 rent schedule. The vacancy and management deductions
   underwriting applies whether or not you self-manage.

5. **Stage 4 — Refinance, where BRRRR actually succeeds or fails**
   - Seasoning: roughly six months of ownership before we lend against current value
   - The two tests: 80% LTV *and* a 1.05x minimum DSCR — the binding one is
     whichever fails first
   - Worked example: $150K purchase plus $50K rehab, $280K appraisal, $224K
     refinance against a $200K basis. Then run the same deal where the DSCR test
     binds instead, and show the capital left in the deal.

6. **Stage 5 — Repeat, and the constraint nobody plans for**
   The seasoning clock, not the capital, sets the pace. Two to three cycles a
   year is realistic; the six-week version sold in courses is not.

7. **The three ways BRRRR deals fail**
   The ARV came in low. The rent did not support the ratio. The rehab ran over
   and the short-term loan matured before the refinance could close.

8. **FAQ (6 questions, FAQPage schema)**
   - Can one loan cover the whole BRRRR cycle?
   - How long before I can refinance?
   - What if the appraisal comes in under my ARV?
   - Do I need the property leased before refinancing?
   - How much capital do I actually get back?
   - How many BRRRR cycles can I run in a year?
