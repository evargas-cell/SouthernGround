## Why this article

`/blog/dscr-loans-explained.html` covers what a DSCR loan *is* and how the ratio
is calculated. This one is narrower and more commercial: the specific thresholds
a borrower has to clear. It should be the page that ranks for "dscr loan
requirements" and every long-tail variant of it.

Cross-link both ways with the existing explainer, and link the product page.

## Outline

1. **The six requirements, up front**
   A summary table the reader can screenshot: minimum DSCR 1.05x, minimum FICO
   660, maximum LTV 85% (lower on cash-out), reserves ~6 months PITIA,
   rent-ready or tenanted condition, LLC closing standard.

2. **Requirement 1 — the ratio**
   1.00 breaks even, 1.25 is the sweet spot, 1.05 is our floor.
   What happens below the floor: reduced leverage or a smaller loan.
   Worked example showing how a $300/month rent difference moves a deal from
   0.965 to 1.126.

3. **Requirement 2 — credit**
   660 minimum. How score bands move rate and maximum leverage.
   Why credit matters more here than on a 12-month fix and flip loan.

4. **Requirement 3 — LTV**
   85% on purchase, lower on cash-out. Explain that LTV and DSCR are two
   independent tests and the binding one is whichever fails first — this is the
   point most competing articles miss.

5. **Requirement 4 — reserves**
   6 months PITIA, what counts as liquid, what does not.

6. **Requirement 5 — property condition**
   Rent-ready or tenanted. Why a rehab property has to go through a fix and flip
   or bridge loan first. The two-step BRRRR sequence.

7. **Requirement 6 — the entity**
   LLC closing, articles, operating agreement, EIN, certificate of good standing.
   Link to the LLC doc checklist.

8. **Seasoning, and what changes on a refinance**
   Roughly six months of ownership before we lend against current appraised value.

9. **The five things borrowers get wrong when self-calculating**
   Gross vs net rent, zero vacancy, no management fee, ignoring HOA, running the
   numbers after going under contract instead of before.

10. **FAQ (6 questions, FAQPage schema)**
    - What is the minimum DSCR ratio to qualify?
    - What credit score do I need for a DSCR loan?
    - How much can I borrow against a rental property?
    - Do I need reserves, and how much?
    - Can I get a DSCR loan on a property that needs work?
    - How long do I have to own a property before refinancing?
